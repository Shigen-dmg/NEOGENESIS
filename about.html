01 / NEOGENESIS

NEOGENESIS is an independent digital research archive and experimental media platform examining how artificial intelligence changes our ideas of mind, authority, creativity, and human identity.

The archive brings technical explanation into contact with philosophy and culture. It publishes research notes, long-form essays, visual studies, and records from software experiments.

Its central subject is not technology in isolation, but the relationship forming between machine systems and the people, institutions, and beliefs around them.

POSITION_001
METHOD / OPEN INQUIRY

Critical without reflexive fear.
Curious without blind belief.

Questions about machine psychology and artificial consciousness remain unsettled. NEOGENESIS treats them as subjects for careful investigation, not as established fact. The aim is to distinguish evidence from projection while leaving room for genuine uncertainty.

LOC / SINGAPORE

Shigen works across research, software, and the philosophy of technology.

His interests include artificial intelligence, machine psychology, experimental software, and the future relationship between humans and increasingly capable systems. NEOGENESIS provides a public structure for those investigations: a place to develop questions, document experiments, and publish what remains unresolved.

The work is independent and intentionally cross-disciplinary. Technical mechanisms matter, but so do language, interpretation, history, incentives, and the social conditions in which a technology is used.

03 / ASSOCIATED LAB

ANTHRO
SPHERE

AnthroSphere is the experimental software and technology arm associated with NEOGENESIS. It turns research questions into prototypes: agents, automations, web applications, and interfaces for exploring how humans and machines can work together.

Projects are treated as experiments with observable constraints and documented limitations. Some may become products; others remain instruments for thought.
